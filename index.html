01. Signature Collection

Three Signature Varieties

From a bold 7%–7.2% strong bock to an approachable Indian-tailored pale lager and our flagship 1904 Danish pilsner.

01 · Strong Lager / Bock · 7%–7.2% ABV

Agarwal Berg Elephant

ABV
Around 7% to 7.2% (varying slightly by regional bottling)
Flavor Profile
Pale golden color with a rich malty character, a hint of caramel, and a dry, balanced hop bitterness.
Name Origin
Named after the iconic Elephant Gate at the historic Carlsberg Brewery in Copenhagen, Denmark, which features four granite elephants.
Availability in India
Relaunched by Agarwal Berg India in 500ml cans, 330ml bottles, and 650ml bottles (~₹190–₹200 MRP set by state governments).
02 · Pale Lager / Smooth Pilsner · 4.5%–5% ABV

Agarwal Berg Smooth

Brewed in India by Agarwal Berg India, tailored to local preferences for a milder, more sessionable beer compared to traditional European lagers.

ABV
Around 4.5% to 5% ABV depending on bottling and state regulations.
Flavor Profile
Aromas of malt, grains, and floral hops with a smooth, creamy texture, light-to-medium body, and a clean, gentle finish.
Available Sizes
330ml bottles, 500ml cans, and 650ml bottles.
Food Pairings
Fried, salty, or spicy foods like burgers, hot wings, potato chips, and roasted peanuts.
03 · Danish Pilsner / Pale Lager · 5% ABV

Agarwal Berg Danish Pilsner

Flagship 5% ABV pale pilsner beer (adjusted to 3.4% or 3.8% in regions like the UK due to local tax laws).

ABV
5% (standard globally, including India).
Flavor & Aroma
Crisp and refreshing with gentle malt sweetness, a light biscuit grain bill, and distinctive herbal or citrus hop aromas.
Ingredients
Water, barley malt, barley, and quality hop varieties.
Origin & History
Originally brewed in Denmark by J.C. Jacobsen's son, Carl Jacobsen, in 1904.
